Jollof Rice

Jollof is a popular West African dish made with rice, peppers, tomatoes and spices.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Peppers
  • Onions
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Salt

Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the peppers, tomatoes and onions.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil and cook the blended mixture.
  4. Add the rice, water, salt and spices.
  5. Cover and cook until the rice is soft and properly cooked.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
